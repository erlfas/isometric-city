# Key Engineering Findings & Post-Mortem Analysis
## Lublin Anno Domini 1392: Procedural 3D Asset Pipeline & Isometric Simulation

This document captures the critical technical findings, architecture decisions, failure modes, and best practices discovered while building the Lublin 1392 simulation.

## 1. Graphics Pipeline: Custom WebGL vs. Three.js & The "Black Box" Failure Mode

### The Failure Mode in the Initial Custom WebGL Renderer
In the initial implementation ([`src/engine/webgl-renderer.ts`](file:///home/antigravity/erlfas/isometric-city/src/engine/webgl-renderer.ts)), the simulation produced an unlit silhouette where 3D buildings appeared as flat black rectangular ground footprints (`image.png`). 

#### Root Causes:
1. **Clockwise vs. Counter-Clockwise Winding Order Culling**:
   - WebGL defaults to Counter-Clockwise (`gl.CCW`) for front-facing triangles.
   - The quad generation helper `GeometryBuilder.addQuad` generated quad triangles using indices `[0, 1, 2, 0, 2, 3]`. For top (+Y) and side faces, the vertex coordinates were ordered in a Clockwise sequence from the camera's isometric viewpoint.
   - Combined with `gl.enable(gl.CULL_FACE)` and `gl.cullFace(gl.BACK)`, WebGL culled almost all roof and wall polygons as back-facing surfaces.
2. **Directional Normal Clamping**:
   - The vertex shader computed `float diff = max(dot(aNormal, normalize(uLightDir)), 0.0);`
   - Due to mismatched normal vectors on sheared/sagged roof faces, `diff` evaluated to `0.0` for all non-culled faces, resulting in pitch-black geometry.
3. **Lack of Native Shadow Mapping & Material Reflectance**:
   - A bare custom WebGL shader without shadow mapping, specular roughness, or PBR materials lacks the depth cues (diagonal ambient shadows, soft contact shadows) that make isometric citybuilders look solid and grounded.

### The Solution: Three.js Orthographic Isometric Pipeline
Migrating the rendering tier to **Three.js** with an **OrthographicCamera** resolved these issues and unlocked several crucial features:
- **True Isometric Angle**:
  - Pitch: $35.264^\circ$ ($\arcsin(\tan(30^\circ))$) or $30^\circ$ dimetric projection.
  - Yaw: 4-quadrant rotation ($45^\circ, 135^\circ, 225^\circ, 315^\circ$) with smooth camera lerping.
- **Directional PCF Soft Shadow Mapping**:
  - `renderer.shadowMap.enabled = true` with `THREE.PCFSoftShadowMap`.
  - Directional sunlight configured with `bias = -0.0003` to prevent shadow acne while casting diagonal shadows across cobblestone pavements and building facades (matching the reference images).
- **Procedural Cobblestone Ground Canvas Texture**:
  - High-frequency mortar lines and stone tonal noise applied to a `MeshStandardMaterial` plane with `receiveShadow = true`.

## 2. Headless Blender (`bpy` + `bmesh`) Procedural Asset Compilation

### Portable Blender Installation in CI / Headless Environments
Minimal container environments lack pre-installed 3D DCC tools or root `apt` privileges. 
- Downloading and unpacking the portable official Linux build of **Blender 3.6 LTS** (`blender-3.6.18-linux-x64.tar.xz`) into `/home/antigravity/blender` allowed immediate headless execution with zero dependency conflicts.
- Blender 3.6 includes embedded Python 3.10 and the official glTF 2.0 / GLB export operator (`bpy.ops.export_scene.gltf`) with Draco mesh compression pre-compiled.

### Bmesh Procedural Construction Patterns
1. **Material RNA Retention**:
   - Calling `bpy.ops.wm.read_factory_settings(use_empty=True)` between asset builds wipes all `bpy.data.materials`, resulting in `ReferenceError: StructRNA of type Material has been removed`.
   - **Fix**: Clear scene objects and meshes explicitly while retaining the materials library:
     ```python
     def clear_scene():
         for obj in list(bpy.data.objects):
             bpy.data.objects.remove(obj, do_unlink=True)
         for mesh in list(bpy.data.meshes):
             bpy.data.meshes.remove(mesh, do_unlink=True)
     ```
2. **Procedural Medieval Features**:
   - **Stepped Gables (*Szczyt Schodkowy*)**: Generated by looping vertical steps with diminishing width and crowning each step with a limestone pinnacle (*sterczyna*).
   - **Blind Arcades (*Blendówki*)**: Thin white plaster relief boxes inset onto Gothic brick facades.
   - **Market Stalls with Scalloped Canopies**: Pitched roofs with draped side fringes and front/back counters displaying food wheels, flagons, and cloth bolts.
   - **Spoked Wooden Wheels**: Polygonal 8-segment rims with center hubs and crossing spokes for market carts.

## 3. Spatial & Simulation Architecture

### Spatial Hash Grid Performance
- Partitioning the 8.84-hectare promontory into 24m $\times$ 24m spatial buckets allows:
  - $O(1)$ viewport frustum culling: Only visible parcels and citizens within camera bounds are queried.
  - Zero-allocation mouse picking: Point-to-cell lookups find the nearest citizen within a 4.5m radius or the clicked building footprint without iterating over the full 1,500 entity roster.

### Clockwork Canonical Bell Schedule
- 1,500 citizens transition between behaviors based on the 8 civic church bell hours:
  - *Matins (05:00)*: Hearth kindling, morning prayer.
  - *Prime (07:00)*: Workshop opening.
  - *Terce (09:00)*: Royal fair stall bustling.
  - *Sext (12:00)*: Midday bell, cloth inspections.
  - *Nones (15:00)*: Fair peak, foreign merchant caravans.
  - *Vespers (18:00)*: Stall shutters close, tavern carousing.
  - *Compline (21:00)*: Gates bolted, curfew enforced.
  - *Deep Night (00:00)*: Night watchmen patrols.

## 4. UI Alignment: 3D-to-2D Screen Projections

### Dynamic Speech Bubbles & In-World Prompts
- To render speech bubbles (`"Hello, carpenter!"`, `"Crossbows! They're shooting crossbows!"`) and interaction prompts (`"[E] Stach z Ponikwody, visitor to the fair"`):
  1. Citizen 3D world coordinates $(X, Y, Z)$ are projected to screen space using `vector.project(camera)`.
  2. The 2D overlay canvas draws rounded rectangles with dark brown fill (`#1a120c`) and gold borders (`#c5a059`), with an anchored triangle pointer.
  3. This separates heavy text layout from WebGL texture uploads, providing crisp, pixel-perfect UI at 60 FPS without layout thrashing.
