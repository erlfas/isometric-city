Material board

Each texture family the city will use, with a swatch cropped from the concept paintings as the target look. Every swatch is a style reference only. The real textures will be flat-lit, seamless and drawn to real-world scale.

What you are signing off

  1. The 16 families below and which existing materials each one replaces.
  2. The real-world size of one texture tile, which fixes how big bricks, logs and shingles look next to a 1.75 m citizen.
  3. The split: 7 hero textures you generate from the prompts, 9 textures I generate in code.
  4. Two material splits in the Blender pipeline: TimberDark becomes log walls vs. structural beams, and Limestone becomes castle ashlar vs. rough fieldstone plinths.

How to generate the hero textures

For each You generate card: attach its swatch from docs/material_board/swatches/ as the style reference image, paste the prompt, and make 2–3 variants.

Save them as PNG at the largest size your tool offers (at least 1024×1024) into texture_sources/<family>/, for example texture_sources/brick_gothic/v1.png.

You don't need to fix seams or colours. I'll pick the best variant, make it tile, match it to the palette, remove leftover baked lighting and derive the normal map.

You generate hero surface, image tool + prompt I generate procedural, in code

Texture families

Stays flat colour

These surfaces are small, shiny or meant to read as a solid accent, so they keep a flat colour and only get the lighting rebalance: IronWrought, IronSteel, GoldSign, GoldPretzel, FireOrange, GlassDark, SootBlack, CoalBlack, water and dye liquids, cloth bolts, goods, hides, bread and pottery.

Technical spec

ItemDecision
Resolution1024×1024 per map (desktop target, no phone constraint)
Maps per familyColour (sRGB) + normal map. Roughness stays a per-material number.
MappingWorld-space box projection at export, so a brick is the same size on every building. Roof textures follow the slope; logs and beams follow their length.
DeliveryShared files in public/textures/, loaded once at runtime and matched by material name. Not embedded in the 31 GLBs.
VariationThe existing per-house colour jitter keeps working as a tint over the texture, cached to about 8 variants per family.
